01 / About

Background

I am a Research Fellow at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, working on AI/ML for multimodal medical imaging and predictive informatics. I apply deep learning models for tumor/organ segmentation and deformable image registration to monitor how tumors and surrounding normal tissues respond during radiotherapy in head and neck and lung cancer patients. By quantifying tumor regression and mass loss, I develop models to predict patient outcomes and guide adaptive radiotherapy.

Before moving into medical imaging, I earned my PhD in experimental high-energy physics at IIT Hyderabad in collaboration with the Belle II Collaboration at KEK, Japan. This work gave me a strong background in large-scale data analysis, Monte Carlo simulation, statistical methods, detector performance studies, and data quality checks.

03 / Projects

Selected AI & data science work

MICCAI 2026 · 1st place internationally

PanSegLight: Pan-Cancer CT Lesion Segmentation

A lightweight, containerized nnU-Net pipeline for automated tumor segmentation in CT across multiple cancer sites, trained on a 17,000+ case multi-institutional dataset and evaluated with a false-positive-weighted Tversky loss.

Longitudinal imaging · MSK

AI-Based Deformable Registration for Adaptive Radiotherapy

Applied a 3D convolutional LSTM U-Net registration framework to weekly imaging in head and neck and lung cancer, extracting mid-treatment changes in CT density, volume, and mass as early response biomarkers (ASTRO 2026 oral).

PET biomarkers · MSK

Toward PET-Guided Adaptive Radiotherapy

Linking pre-treatment FDG PET uptake to the speed of tumor volume regression on weekly CBCT in head and neck cancer and locally advanced NSCLC, to identify patients who may benefit from adaptive replanning.
