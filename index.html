01 / About

Background

Lopamudra is a postdoctoral research fellow in medical physics at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, with a background in experimental particle physics, data science, and statistical analysis, and expertise in quantitative imaging and deep learning. Her work focuses on fine-tuning and applying deep learning to image registration, segmentation, and quantitative feature extraction from longitudinal CT, PET, and MRI. Her research aims to support cancer research and precision oncology by translating quantitative imaging and AI-based methods into clinically meaningful tools for early assessment of treatment response and personalized, digital-twin-guided radiotherapy treatment planning in head and neck cancer.
