01 / About

Background

Lopamudra is an AI/ML scientist and research associate at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center with expertise in multimodal medical imaging, deep learning, quantitative image analysis, and predictive modeling. Her work focuses on developing scalable pipelines for CT, PET, and MRI; fine-tuning pretrained models for image registration and segmentation; extracting anatomical, metabolic, and deformation-based biomarkers; and integrating imaging features with clinical outcomes for treatment-response assessment. Her PhD research in experimental high-energy physics provided extensive experience in Python/C++, supervised machine learning, Monte Carlo simulation, statistical estimation, uncertainty quantification, and analysis of large-scale experimental datasets.
